1. INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Welcome to PDF Saathi ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are 
committed to protecting it. PDF Saathi is designed as a privacy-focused, 
100% offline PDF reader and document management tool for Android devices.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates, why certain device 
permissions are requested, and our strict commitment to protecting your personal 
information and document privacy.

By installing and using PDF Saathi, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA COLLECTION AND USAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PDF Saathi operates completely OFFLINE.

• Zero Data Collection: We do NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any 
  personal information, user identity, IP address, device ID, or location data.
• Document Privacy: Any PDF file or document opened, viewed, or processed 
  within PDF Saathi remains exclusively on your device. We do NOT upload or 
  transfer your files to any remote server or cloud service.
• Local Storage Only: App settings, recent file history, and bookmarks are 
  saved locally on your device using Android's encrypted local database (Room/SQLite) 
  and are never transmitted externally.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DEVICE PERMISSIONS REQUIRED & PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To function as a document viewer and manager, PDF Saathi requests access to 
the following storage permissions:

1. READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_DOCUMENTS:
   Allows the app to locate, list, and open PDF documents stored on your 
   device storage.

2. MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE:
   Required to provide full document management features across your device 
   (such as organizing, renaming, deleting, or scanning PDF documents across 
   all storage locations).

All permissions are used strictly for local file operations on your device. 
No data accessed via these permissions is ever collected or sent outside your device.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & ANALYTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PDF Saathi does NOT use any third-party tracking, advertising, or telemetry SDKs.

• No Advertising: We do not display third-party advertisements or use ad networks.
• No Analytics: We do not include Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, 
  Crashlytics, or tracking cookies.
• No Internet Access: PDF Saathi does not request network/internet permission 
  in its Android manifest, making it impossible for the app to send data over the internet.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DATA SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Since PDF Saathi operates entirely offline and does not transmit data over the 
internet, your documents and personal data remain protected by Android's built-in 
application sandboxing and your device's security system (passcode, biometric lock, 
encryption). We recommend keeping your Android OS updated to ensure maximum security.
