ABOUT PDF SAATHI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Welcome to PDF Saathi – Your ultimate offline companion for reading, managing, 
and organizing PDF documents on Android!

PDF Saathi was created with a clear vision: to provide Android users with a fast, 
clean, lightweight, and privacy-first PDF utility app. Whether you are a student, 
working professional, researcher, or everyday user, PDF Saathi makes handling 
PDF files smooth and effortless.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY CHOOSE PDF SAATHI?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 100% Offline & Private:
  Your documents belong to you. PDF Saathi operates entirely offline without 
  requiring an active internet connection or uploading files to external servers.

• Instant Document Scanning & Access:
  Easily auto-detect and list all PDF files stored across your device storage.

• Fast & Smooth Reading:
  Enjoy single-page, continuous scroll, dark mode reading, and fast page navigation.

• Password Protected PDF Support:
  Seamlessly unlock and view password-encrypted PDF files on the go.

• Organization & Bookmarking:
  Bookmark essential pages, view recent documents, and manage your PDF files 
  with ease.

• Zero Ads & Clean UI:
  Designed with modern Jetpack Compose material UI for a clutter-free reading 
  experience.
